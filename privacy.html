Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

CloudDrive2 is a personal-use application operated solely by its owner.

  • Purpose: The application uses the Google Drive API, with the owner's authorization through Google OAuth, to copy the owner's own Google Drive files to the owner's home NAS for backup.
  • Data handling: File transfers are performed by the CloudDrive2 software running on the owner's own NAS, directly between Google Drive and the NAS. No files or credentials pass through any third-party server.
  • No collection or sharing: The application does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information with any third party. It is not used by anyone other than the owner.
  • Revoking access: Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions).
