CloudDrive2

CloudDrive2 is a personal-use application. It connects to my own Google Drive through Google OAuth and copies my files one-way to my own home NAS (a Feiniu / fnOS EVO 2) for backup purposes.

The app is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public and collects no data from any other person.
